# Beam Analysis under Uniformly Distributed Load (UDL)

## Objective
To analyze the structural behavior of a beam subjected to a uniformly distributed load using ANSYS.

## Geometry
A straight beam model was created with appropriate dimensions.

## Material Properties
- Material: Structural Steel

## Boundary Conditions
- Fixed Support applied at one end
- Uniformly Distributed Load applied along the beam
- Additional force/displacement constraints applied where required

## Meshing
- Fine mesh generated for accurate stress distribution

## Results

### 1. Equivalent (Von Mises) Stress
- Maximum stress observed at: (mention location)
- Stress concentration near fixed support

### 2. Directional Deformation
- Maximum deformation at free end
- Deformation follows expected bending pattern

## Conclusion
The simulation results align with theoretical expectations:
- Maximum stress occurs near the fixed support
- Maximum deformation occurs at the free end

This validates the correctness of boundary conditions and load application.

- Understanding of FEA workflow in ANSYS
- Importance of boundary conditions
- Interpretation of stress and deformation results
